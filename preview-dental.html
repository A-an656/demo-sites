FAQ

Common questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

No. With modern anaesthesia and rotary instruments, most patients feel no more than a regular filling.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, implants can last for decades.

Do you offer EMI?

Yes, EMI options are available for implants, aligners, braces and smile makeovers.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We offer gentle paediatric dentistry, including cavity care and preventive treatments.
